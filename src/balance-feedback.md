# Balance Feedback

*As of March 29th, 2026*<br>

Recipe and T.U.R.D. balance feedback should be reported in the [recipe balance megathread](https://github.com/pyanodon/pybugreports/issues/696).

Use that thread when your feedback is one of these:

- "Why does this recipe exist? I would never use this."
- "This recipe is too strong, I would never use the alternatives."
- "This TURD upgrade is too weak. Who would choose this?"

Keep reports concise and include:

- The internal name of the recipe or T.U.R.D. path. In game, hover the recipe and press `Ctrl+Shift+F`.
- Why you think it is underpowered or overpowered.
- What change you suggest.

Use this format:

```md
Internal name: `example-recipe`
Problem: This option is strictly worse than the alternatives because ...
Suggested change: ...
```

If your report needs a long explanation, open a dedicated issue instead of adding an essay to the megathread.
